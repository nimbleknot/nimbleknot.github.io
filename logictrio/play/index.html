Logic Trio is in Google Play’s closed test before it goes public. Anyone with an Android phone (Android 7 or newer) and a Google account can join and play it now. Please keep the group’s emails on: at most one or two a week, and it is how you hear about updates and when the test is over.

iPhone: Logic Trio is Android only for now.

Use one Google account for all three steps: the one your Play Store uses (open the Play Store and tap your picture, top right). A personal Gmail works best; work and school accounts are often blocked from joining groups.

Why a test

Google Play asks new developers for at least 12 testers who stay in the test for 14 days in a row before a game can go public. Please stay in the group and keep the game installed until we post that the test is over (about 3–4 weeks), and play whenever you like. And please don’t tap the ads to help — Google treats that as fraud and can close our account. Every kind of feedback is welcome at nimbleknot.games@gmail.com.

On a computer

Steps 1 and 2 work in a computer’s browser too. Then install from the Play Store on your Android phone, signed in to the same Google account.

What it is

Crowns: one crown in every row, column and colour, and crowns never touch.

Suns & Moons: as many suns as moons in every row and column, never three alike in a row.

Paths: one line through every cell, passing the numbers in order.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution. Solve one a day to keep your streak. No lives, and nothing counts down. Works offline. Free, with ads, and no ad ever interrupts a puzzle.
