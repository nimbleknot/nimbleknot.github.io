Open in Google Play

Opened this inside LinkedIn, Discord, Instagram or another app, and the button shows a web page instead of the Play Store? Tap ⋮ (or the share icon), choose Open in Chrome or Open in browser, and tap the button again. Searching the Play Store works too: look for Logic Trio: Daily Puzzles by Nimbleknot (other games share the name).

On a computer

The button opens Google Play in your browser. Sign in with the Google account your phone uses, choose Install, and pick your phone.

What it is

Crowns: one crown in every row, column and colour, and crowns never touch.

Suns & Moons: as many suns as moons in every row and column, never three alike in a row.

Paths: one line through every cell, passing the numbers in order.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution. Solve one a day to keep your streak. No lives, and nothing counts down. Works offline. Free, with ads, and no ad ever interrupts a puzzle.
