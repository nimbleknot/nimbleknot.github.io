Today's Paths puzzle

A number path puzzle: one line through every cell. A new board every day at midnight, your time. Free, in any browser, no sign-up.

How to play

  • Start on 1 and drag through every number in order.
  • The line fills every cell exactly once and ends on the last number.
  • A thick wall between two cells cannot be crossed.
  • Drag back along the line to take steps back.
