Today's Suns & Moons puzzle

A binary puzzle, also called Takuzu or binairo. A new board every day at midnight, your time. Free, in any browser, no sign-up.

How to play

  • Tap a cell for a sun, again for a moon, a third time to empty it.
  • Every row and every column has as many suns as moons.
  • No three suns or three moons next to each other in a row or column.
  • An = between two cells means they match; an × means they differ.
