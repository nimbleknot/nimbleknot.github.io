Delete your Logic Trio data

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Logic Trio is a puzzle game made by Nimbleknot. It has no account, no sign-in and no server, so there is no profile of you anywhere to close. Everything the game remembers is on your own phone, and you can erase all of it yourself in a few seconds.

How to delete everything, in the game

Open Logic Trio → tap Me → tap Settings → scroll to the bottom → tap Delete my data.

It asks you to confirm. When you do, the game becomes new: it opens as if you had just installed it.

You do not need to ask anyone, wait for anything, or be online. The deletion happens on the phone, immediately, and it cannot be undone.

Uninstalling the game does the same thing. Android removes the whole of it with the app.

What that deletes

All of it, immediately and permanently:

  • every puzzle you have solved, your best times and your stars
  • your streak, your freezes, your gold days and your monthly trophies
  • your hints
  • your settings — theme, language, sound, vibration, colour-blind patterns, how you place a crown, your reminder time
  • your scheduled daily reminders, which are cancelled on the phone
  • the small diagnostic log the game keeps on the phone
  • any half-finished board

What is kept, and by whom

Nimbleknot keeps nothing. There is no server and no database, so there is no copy of your progress anywhere but your phone, and nothing is retained after you delete it — there is no retention period because there is nothing retained.

Two things are not Nimbleknot's to delete, and it is only fair to say so plainly:

1. Your own Google Drive backup

If Android's automatic backup is switched on, your phone may have copied the game's save file to your own Google Drive, the same way it backs up your other apps. That copy is in your Google account, not ours, and we cannot reach it. You can remove it in your phone's settings, under Google → Backup, or by turning backup off for Logic Trio.

2. What Google's advertising and analytics services hold

The game shows adverts and reports crashes through Google's own services. They receive technical information — an advertising identifier, an approximate area from your IP address, and records of what happened in the game. None of it identifies you by name, and Nimbleknot never receives it: it goes to Google. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what.

You control that side directly on your phone:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android → Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops adverts being personalised to your device.
  • If you live in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or certain US states, the game shows you Google's consent form and you can change your answer at any time in Me → Settings → Your data → Privacy choices.

If you would rather ask

Write to nimbleknot.games@gmail.com and say what you would like removed. Bear in mind what is above: because Nimbleknot holds no database and receives nothing that identifies you, there is usually nothing held about you for us to find or erase — the deletion is genuinely in your hands, on your phone. Questions are welcome all the same and will be answered.
