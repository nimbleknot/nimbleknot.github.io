How to play Binary Puzzles (Suns & Moons)

Balance every row and column

Suns & Moons is a binary puzzle: every cell holds a sun or a moon.

The rules

  1. As many suns as moons in every row and column.
  2. No three alike in a row.
  3. Between cells: = same, × different.
  4. Tap once for a sun, twice for a moon, three times to clear. Grey tiles are fixed.
  5. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and you never have to guess.

A solved example

Tips for beginners

  • Two alike side by side force the third: the cells at both ends of the pair take the other symbol.
  • A gap between two alike, like sun, empty, sun, must be the other symbol.
  • Count as you go: once a row of six has three suns, the rest of it is moons.
