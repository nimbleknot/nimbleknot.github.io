In one line

Logic Trio is a free Android game with a new page of three logic puzzles every day, made by one person in India.

Short description

Logic Trio gives you a new page of three logic puzzles every day, the same page for every player in the world. Crowns, Suns & Moons and Paths each have exactly one solution. Each game also has 1,200 levels in four difficulties. Free on Android, with ads, in seven languages.

Long description

Logic Trio: Daily Puzzles is a calm logic puzzle game for Android. Every day it opens a new page of three puzzles, the same page for every player in the world.

Crowns: one crown in every row, column and colour, and no two crowns touch. It is a star battle puzzle. Suns & Moons: as many suns as moons in every row and column, never three alike in a row. It is a binary puzzle. Paths: draw one line through the numbers in order, filling every cell. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

Solve one to keep your streak, all three for a gold day. Each game also has 1,200 levels in four difficulties, all open from the start. Hints show where to look, and nothing counts down. Every day there is also a world race on today’s page: your time against everyone who solved the same puzzle. A home-screen widget shows today’s page and your streak. Free with ads, in seven languages.

Fact sheet

Developer
Nimbleknot, a one-person studio in India. The developer is Ritesh.
Platform
Android 7 or newer, on Google Play. There is no iPhone version.
Released
11 October 2026, in 178 countries.
Price
Free, paid for by ads. No ad interrupts a puzzle.
Languages
English, Hindi, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish (Latin America), Indonesian, German, French.
The three puzzles
Crowns (a star battle puzzle), Suns & Moons (a binary puzzle), Paths (a number path puzzle).
The daily page
Three new puzzles every day, the same page for every player in the world, kept on a monthly calendar.
Levels
1,200 in each game, 3,600 in all, in four difficulties, all open from the start. After the last one, the phone makes more.
Help
Unlimited undo and redo, a free Check, and hints that show where to look.
Nothing counts down
No lives, no energy, nothing to run out of. An optional stopwatch counts up.
Offline
The puzzles work with no internet. Only the ads need a connection.
Size
Under 25 MB to download.
Sign-in
Optional. Google Play Games keeps progress across phones and adds achievements and world leaderboards, among them a daily race on today’s page.

From the developer

I’m Ritesh, and Nimbleknot is just me, in India. Logic Trio is my first game. I made it for a few calm minutes a day: three puzzles, one answer each, and nothing rushing you.

I’m not a programmer, so AI helped me write the code. The puzzles come from our own generator, and every puzzle is checked to have exactly one solution.
